# Privacy Policy — LaTeX render

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

**LaTeX render** is a browser extension that renders LaTeX / mathematical
notation locally inside supported web chat pages.

## Data collection

**This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data.**

- All LaTeX detection and rendering happens entirely **on your device**,
  inside your browser.
- The extension makes **no network requests**. The MathJax rendering engine,
  math fonts, and all other resources are bundled inside the extension itself.
- Your messages, the formulas you view, your browsing activity, and any
  personal information **never leave your computer** and are **never sent to
  us or to any third party**.

## Permissions

The extension requests only what it needs to render math locally:

- **`scripting`** — to inject the bundled MathJax renderer into supported chat
  pages so that LaTeX text can be converted into rendered SVG math.
- **`clipboardWrite`** — to copy a formula's original LaTeX source to your
  clipboard when you click a rendered formula.
- **Host access** to specific chat domains (Element, Telegram, Discord,
  OpenClaw, Hermes, and developer tunnel hosts) — so the renderer can scan
  page text for math on those sites. All processing is local.

## Third parties

There are no third-party services, analytics, trackers, or advertising of any
kind in this extension.
